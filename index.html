Welcome to KWYO

We are Waterloo Region’s comprehensive orchestral training program, a non-profit continuing a proud legacy that began in 1966. We inspire children and youth ages 6 to 22 to grow through high-standard orchestral instruction and enriching performance experiences in a fun, supportive, and safe environment.

Prospective members join through auditions, most held in late spring. KWYO showcases young musicians’ talents and achievements in the main concerts, community performances and collaborations.

The Kitchener Waterloo Youth Orchestra acknowledges that we operate on the traditional territory of the Attawandaron (Neutral), Anishinaabeg, and Haudenosaunee peoples. We honor their enduring stewardship of the land and waters, and we are grateful for the knowledge they continue to share. As an organization dedicated to youth, music, and community, we are committed to ongoing learning and supporting reconciliation in meaningful ways.
